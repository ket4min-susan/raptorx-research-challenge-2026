## 1. What It Is

**Pyth Network** (ticker: **$PYTH**) is a decentralized oracle network that provides real-time, high-fidelity price data for blockchain applications. Unlike traditional oracle solutions that push data continuously on-chain, Pyth employs a **pull-based oracle model**, publishing high-frequency price data to its own application chain (appchain) and allowing consumers to "pull" prices onto their target blockchain only when needed. This design significantly reduces gas costs and improves efficiency for DeFi protocols.

The project operates on the **Solana blockchain**, with its primary trading pair being **PYTH/Wrapped SOL** on **Raydium** (a Solana-based decentralized exchange). The contract address for the token is `HZ1JovNiVvGrGNiiYvEozEVgZ58xaU3RKwX8eACQBCt3`.

Key characteristics of Pyth Network include:
- **First-party data sources**: Price data comes directly from trading firms, market makers, and exchanges rather than third-party aggregators
- **Aggregation algorithm**: Designed to prevent a small number of outlier publishers from skewing final prices, with each publisher's confidence interval factoring into their submission weight
- **Pyth Pro**: A subscription product targeting traditional finance's estimated $50 billion market data industry, reportedly crossing $1 million in annual recurring revenue shortly after launch
- **Fixed token supply**: All 10 billion PYTH tokens were minted at launch with no further issuance, though 85% were initially locked with scheduled releases

The token's official website is [pyth.network](https://pyth.network), with active communities on [Twitter](https://twitter.com/PythNetwork), [Telegram](https://telegram.me/Pyth_Network), and [Discord](https://discord.com/invite/PythNetwork).
